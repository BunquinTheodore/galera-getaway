TRAVEL GUIDE

Getting to Puerto Galera

LOGISTICS MAP

Manila to Puerto Galera

Most travelers take a bus to Batangas Pier, then a ferry across to Mindoro.

  1. Manila

    Board a bus at PITX, Cubao or Buendia heading to Batangas Pier.

  2. Batangas Pier

    About 2 to 3 hours by bus. Pay the terminal fee and board your ferry.

  3. Ferry

    Fast craft takes about 1 hour. RoRo takes a little over 2 hours.

  4. Balatero Port

    Arrive in Puerto Galera. Take a tricycle or van to your beach.

IMPORTANT REMINDERS

Fees and requirements

  • Terminal fees. About PHP 90 at Batangas Pier and PHP 30 at Balatero.
  • Environmental user's fee (PG Pass). PHP 120 per visitor, PHP 50 for Mindoro residents. Seniors, PWDs and students over six with valid ID get 20 percent off.
  • Register early. Pay at the port or register online in advance.
  • Bring valid ID. Needed to claim fee discounts.
  • Rainy season. July to October brings strong winds and waves. Swimming may be unsafe in some months.
  • Cash. The ATM can run out of cash in peak season. Bring enough pesos.
  • Roads. Roads are steep and winding. Ride a motorbike only with experience.
  • Local rules. Follow local government unit requirements for tourists and boat tours.
