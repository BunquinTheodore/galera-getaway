THINGS TO DO

Popular activities

Diving

Scuba diving

USD 26 to 32 per single tank dive

Around 35 dive shops operate here, and most dive sites are a short boat ride from Sabang. Single tank dives include equipment, boat and guide.

Boat tour

Island hopping

PHP 400 per person

Boat tours leave from Minolo Port and visit three beaches of your choice.

Nature

Waterfall visits

Fees vary check at the gate

Tukuran Falls in San Teodoro and Tamaraw Falls toward Calapan. Entrance fees may apply.
